About Naekana Sacco

Leading light in transport and logistics since the 1970s

Our History

Naekana Route 134 Sacco Society Limited, popularly known as Naekana Sacco, is a Kenyan public service transport company. The company is one of the longest-serving matatu operators in the country, having been in operation for more than 4 decades.

A trip to downtown Nairobi near OTC, one would not miss the long line of white vehicles with blue and orange stripes offloading passengers while others take off.

Naekana Route 134 Sacco Society Limited was founded in the 1970s. Initially, it operated Peugeot 504 station wagons. In the 1990s, a group of members formed an organization called Namanga Nissan Services operating Nissan QD's. In early 2000, a group of 27 members came up with the idea of forming the Sacco, and on November 23, 2003, it was officially registered as Naekana Route 134 Sacco Society Limited under the Ministry of Marketing and Co-operative Development.

Today, the Sacco has a fleet of over 260 vehicles.
