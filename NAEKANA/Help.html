Frequently asked

How do I book a seat online?

Visit our Payment page, select your departure and destination, choose your travel time and number of seats, then submit. You will receive an e-receipt after payment confirmation.

What is the fare from Laset to Nairobi?

The fare is Ksh 1,000 per seat for the Laset (Illasit) → Nairobi route. Other routes have different fares — check the fare table on the booking page.

How do I send a parcel with Naekana?

Go to the Payment page, switch to the "Send a Parcel" tab, fill in the parcel details, sender and receiver information (including ID numbers), and submit. Our team will contact you.

What items are not allowed in parcels?

We do not transport: Animals, all kinds of fertilizers, and any kind of explosive. Please confirm your parcel complies before sending.

I left my item on a Naekana vehicle. What should I do?

Please report it immediately using the form on this page (select "Lost item on vehicle") or call +254 700 807 964. Provide your travel details and a description of the item.

How do I join the Sacco or apply for a loan?

Visit our head office at Laset town, Route 134, or contact us via the Contact page. We offer savings and loan services with over Ksh 155 million in member deposits.

How long does it take to get a response?

Our support team typically responds within 24 hours during working hours. For urgent matters, please call our hotline directly.

Need urgent help?

Our support line is open during working hours — reach out anytime.

+254 700 807 964 support@naekana.co.ke
