Our Services

Leading light in transport and logistics

Transport

We offer reliable and comfortable transport services from Illasit and surrounding areas to different locations across Kenya and Tanzania. Our modern fleet ensures a safe and pleasant journey for all passengers.

Book your seat easily online — choose your departure time and preferred vehicle, select the number of seats, make payment, and receive your e-receipt shortly.

Our experienced drivers and well-maintained vehicles guarantee punctuality and comfort on every trip.

Courier Services

Send and receive goods from different places around Kenya and Tanzania using Naekana courier services — not only cheap but also reliable. We handle your parcels with care and ensure timely delivery.

For safety and regulatory compliance, we do not transport the following items:
  • Animals
  • All kinds of fertilizers
  • Any kind of explosive

Savings & Loan Services

Naekana Sacco offers long-term, emergency, development, and normal loans to members. The sum of the loan is dependent on the amount of savings.
